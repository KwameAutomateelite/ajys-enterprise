The brand behind our shelves

Interplast Limited is West Africa's leading producer of plastic pipe systems. Established in Accra in 1970 and fully Ghanaian owned, it manufactures on Spintex Road to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 and ISO 14001:2015 standards and exports to more than 22 African countries.

Panelast PVC tongue & groove panels

Panelast PVC T&G wall and ceiling panels are made by Interplast. The tongue and groove edges lock together with no visible fixings, so a ceiling goes up in hours rather than days, and the finished surface never needs painting.

  • Moisture and termite resistant, so no sagging or staining in humid rooms.
  • Wipe-clean surface for kitchens, bathrooms, shops and verandas.
  • Lightweight panels that cut and fit with basic hand tools.
  • Available in standard lengths with matching trims and corners.

Steel security doors

Reinforced steel security doors for main entrances, compound gates, offices and shops. Each door comes with a multi-point locking system, a steel frame and a powder-coated finish that stands up to the weather.

  • Single and double-leaf doors in standard and custom sizes.
  • Multi-point locks with anti-drill cylinders and solid hinges.

Frequently asked questions

Is A'Jys Enterprise an Interplast dealer?

Yes. A'Jys Enterprise is a direct dealer for Interplast products, based on Spintex Road in Accra, Ghana. We supply genuine Interplast pipes and fittings, Panelast PVC T&G panels and Everlast uPVC windows and doors.

Where is A'Jys Enterprise located?

A'Jys Enterprise is on Spintex Road, Accra, in the Greater Accra Region of Ghana. Call or WhatsApp +233 54 625 6657 for directions and opening times.

Which Interplast pipes can I buy from A'Jys Enterprise?

We supply Interplast uPVC pressure pipes and fittings, uPVC borehole casings and screens, HDPE PE100 pressure pipes in sizes up to 1,200 mm, PPR pipes and fittings for hot and cold water, Incor corrugated pipes for drainage, and cable duct systems.

Do you sell PVC T&G ceiling and wall panels?

Yes. We stock Panelast PVC tongue and groove (T&G) panels made by Interplast for ceilings and walls. They fit together without nails showing, never need painting and are not affected by humidity.

Do you supply Everlast uPVC windows and doors?

Yes. We supply Everlast uPVC windows in sliding, casement, fixed and asymmetric designs, and Everlast uPVC doors in sliding, tilt and slide and turn designs. Frames are fusion welded, multi-chambered and reinforced with steel. Ask us about measurement and installation for your project.

What security doors do you offer?

We supply steel security doors with multi-point locking for homes, offices and compound entrances. Sizes, finishes and lock options are confirmed when you request a quote.

How do I get a quote from A'Jys Enterprise?

Send your product list with sizes and quantities to +233 54 625 6657 on WhatsApp, call the same number, or use the quote form on this website. We confirm stock and reply with a priced quote.

Do you deliver outside Accra?

Delivery across Accra and Tema can be arranged, and pickup is available on Spintex Road. For other regions of Ghana, contact us with your location and order size and we will confirm options.

Do you sell to contractors and plumbers in bulk?

Yes. We supply contractors, plumbers, developers and estate projects with bulk and phased orders of Interplast pipes, fittings, panels, windows and doors. Share your bill of quantities for project pricing.
